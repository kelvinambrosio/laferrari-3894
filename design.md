## Catalog extension
Upper-left magnifier opens a dark full-height search dialog. Curated selection of 23 models, not an exhaustive historical database. Search by model, year, engine and category. Selecting a model updates hero, performance, history and source. Only 458 has a verified local 3D file; other entries show an actual photograph when available or an explicit editorial placeholder. Hide paint/orbit controls outside the 458. Photo credits link to Wikimedia file pages and Wikipedia reference articles.
